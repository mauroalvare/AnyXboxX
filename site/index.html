Can it run GTA VI on PC?

It runs.

The Xbox Series build (1.0.2.1) starts, plays its cutscenes and reaches the open world on a PC, through VIctory, which AnyXboxX includes. It is still experimental: about 16–17 fps in the world on a high-end PC, with some graphics missing and ray tracing off.

Grand Theft Auto VI for Xbox Series, build 1.0.2.1 · Status as of 26 November 2026

Where things stand

  • Boot and menus Observed. The profile screen, first-run settings and pause menu work.
  • Cutscenes and open world Observed. The cutscenes play in full and the player moves through the world.
  • Frame rate In progress. The processor is the bottleneck: it emulates the console's unified memory, through copies and read-backs.
  • Graphics In progress. Ray tracing is off, so indoor and shadowed areas are dark. Some graphics are missing, and objects can turn black briefly while textures stream in.
  • Linux with Proton Experimental. A 23-minute run reached Chapter 1 on Fedora 44 with a Radeon RX 9070 XT and a patched GE-Proton11-12. A later 20-minute Neptune run kept the Chapter 1 boat afloat with ocean readback enabled. Linux notes

The measurements of each earlier build are listed in docs/progress.md.

Measured performance

Measured by the maintainer on 26 November 2026 with Windows 11, a Core i7-13700KF, a GeForce RTX 4070 SUPER and 32 GB of RAM, in Series X mode with a warmed shader cache.

  • Median in the world

    16–17 fps

    Up from 13.4 fps on the morning of 26 November, after three pull requests on the copies and the memory scan (#358, #386, #394).

  • Picture-first mode

    7–9 fps

    The launcher's Quality preset: better lighting and textures, fewer texture refusals, lower frame rate (measured on 25 November).

  • Memory in play

    28–30 GB

    Used by the game process. A 12 GB graphics card runs short of texture memory; the launcher has a preset for it.

A 13-minute Neptune run on 25 November measured 13.8 fps median in the world at 4K with a warmed shader cache. 25 November test notes

What you need

  • Your own copy of the Xbox Series package, version 1.0.2.1, with its game_scarlett_master.exe. Nothing here provides it or explains how to get it.
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, an AVX2 processor, and a Direct3D 12 graphics card with shader model 6.6 and mesh shaders (NVIDIA RTX 20 series or newer, AMD RDNA 2 or newer).
  • 32 GB of RAM recommended, and more than 30 GB free on the system drive: the shader cache alone reached about 13 GB in one precompilation run.
  • Rust and the Visual Studio C++ Build Tools, to build VIctory the first time you play.
